# The Big Glitch — Scene Bible, Scenes 1–20

*Engine-independent scene specifications: what each scene IS — location, POV,
purpose, interaction grammar, hotspots, cues, clip moment — with zero
rendering assumptions. The graphics engine and chosen design treatment
(sodium / twilight / petrol, see DESIGN.md §4) get applied to these specs
later. Format per scene:*

> **ID · Title** — POV · location · in-fiction time
> **Purpose** (story function; Truth-Stack layer it serves)
> **Play** (core interaction / puzzle, target duration)
> **Hotspots/props** (the load-bearing ones; every scene also gets full
> examine-layer coverage per COMEDY.md)
> **Cues** (narrator beats) · **Clip** (the Moments-engine tag)

Chapter Zero scenes implement `story/INTRO.md`; the intro-convergence
decision (NARRATIVE.md §15 Q2b) may retint the TERMINATE targets.

## CHAPTER ONE — "Whatever." (the morning after, and the first threads)

**11 · C1-2 The Claims Case** — Mo · insurer's hollowed-out office floor ·
