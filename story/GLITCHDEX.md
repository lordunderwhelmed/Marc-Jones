# The Glitchdex — documented AI failures & uncanny incidents, ranked and mapped

*A database of real, sourced incidents (2012–2026) for adaptation into The
Big Glitch. Every entry in the source research carries URLs and dates
(preserved in RESEARCH links below). The game fictionalizes names per
NARRATIVE.md §14 — this file keeps the real record as reference. Scores:
**U** = uncanny/emotional charge, **A** = adaptability into a scene/case.*

## The two satirical engines (use these to sort everything)

1. **"The AI was people all along"** — the Mechanical Turk pattern:
   Builder.ai (~700 engineers behind "Natasha," $1.5B valuation → insolvency
   2025), Amazon Just Walk Out (~1,000 reviewers in India), Presto drive-thru
   AI (70% human intervention, per its own SEC filings), Facebook M / x.ai's
   "Amy Ingram" (humans approving every reply), GM Cruise (remote assist
   every 2.5–5 miles). **This is Truth Stack Layer 2 with citations.** The
   Casebook's mid-season arc: case after case ends with a human where the
   AI should be, until the pattern is undeniable.
2. **"The people believed the AI all along"** — the phantom-parade pattern:
   Dublin's AI-invented Halloween parade (thousands lined O'Connell Street
   for nothing, 2024), Glasgow's Willy Wonka Experience, Avianca's fake
   precedents, the Arup deepfake board call ($25M). Belief as the product.

**The thesis the database proves** (and the game's tonal law): the same
mechanic — simulating a person from their data — is a *joke* in the corporate
column and a *wound* in the grief column. That's the whole game in one
observation. Comedy rules (COMEDY.md) apply to column one; the Travis
register applies to column two.

## What this database proves structurally

Nearly every incident has a **discoverable, mundane root cause** — a config
change, a regulator letter, a Reddit joke fossilized in training data, an
SEO payout scheme, an office of 700 people. That is exactly the Root-Cause
Board's grammar. The Casebook doesn't need invented absurdities; it needs
light fictionalization of the public record. Reality is already writing
season two.
